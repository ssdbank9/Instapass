## Requirements and implementation

- Canva/Cursor: remove the single12months box completely; retain a plain Annual plan label, price, upfront term and buy action. Multi-option products still show their selectable durations.
- Claude customer expiry: date only, without00:00 or timezone-offset text. Administrative cycle preview retains precise time/timezone; saved private timestamps retain precision.
- New Product Manager: grouped per-family forms edit duration count/unit, full-term or monthly-rate price entry, USD/GBP/EUR base/sale amounts, automatic or fixed PKR and active/deactivated state. A live browser preview shows upfront totals/monthly equivalents. Existing sale schedules are preserved; advanced stock/logo/coupon settings stay linked.
- Add a duration directly under its family. New backend offer inherits logo/access/category settings, gets a unique SKU and is mapped to the same storefront tile. Duplicate normalized durations are rejected. Day/week terms display full-term prices without an unsupported monthly official-price comparison.
- Edit duration on an existing offer: customer labels follow its stored duration rather than the old hardcoded1/6/12month key. Mapping keys remain stable selection IDs. Same-price duration edits change the checkout quote hash; saved order items remain snapshots.
- Deactivate one offer without hiding its active siblings. The public tile chooses another published representative if the original parent is deactivated. All inactive means no public family tile.
- New products created through the existing advanced editor now get a new grouped tile automatically when they define a genuinely new managed family. Unlisted saved products can be selected in Product Manager, prepared as drafts and reviewed before activation. Historical product/order rows are retained.
- ChatGPT Credits: one group form edits access duration and base price percentage across all19balances. It explicitly applies the same numeric base prices in USD/GBP/EUR and restores automatic PKR; individual advanced overrides remain available in Products & Offers. The storefront duration label follows the saved term.
- Claude Credits: calendar-month or fixed1–31day batch tenure, monthly start day, five editable minimum-day thresholds and percentages, and sales pause. Defaults stay calendar month/start day1/default40/35/30/25/20rates. A fixed-day batch that expires before the next monthly start cannot sell during the gap. FAQ day labels follow custom thresholds. Default monthly renewal behaviour is unchanged.
- Nonce, capability, stale-fingerprint and save-lock checks protect writes. Core product/mapping saves use transactions. New offers can remain drafts until their positive USD price and activation are approved. Very small positive credit amounts that round to zero are unavailable in that currency rather than silently free.

## Owner steps

Open [Product Manager](https://instapass.online/wp-admin/admin.php?page=instapass-product-manager). Find the product, change its duration and prices, then Save this duration. Default entry is exact full-term amounts; choose Monthly rates before entering new monthly amounts. Review the live upfront preview. Use Add a duration for a new option on the same tile. Untick Active and ready to buy to deactivate that offer. Use Prepare existing product for a retired listing, or Add a new product / advanced editor for a new family/logo.

For Claude, open [Claude Credit Tenure & Pricing](https://instapass.online/wp-admin/admin.php?page=instapass-credit-cycle), choose Calendar month or Fixed number of days, edit start day, band minimum days and percentages, then Save tenure and rates. Existing orders retain their purchased expiry. For ChatGPT credit access duration and group percentage, use its Product Manager card.
