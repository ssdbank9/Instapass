## Subscription wording proposed

For Claude Pro, Claude Max5x, Claude Ultra and ChatGPT Plus:

1. Private access to your selected plan for the duration shown.
2. Activation link or instructions sent to your chosen email.
3. Activation within45minutes after payment is verified.
4. Replacement or refund for eligible delivery or activation issues under our Refund Policy.

Instapass's existing policy covers eligible failed, late or mismatched delivery/access. The draft does not invent a seven-day/full-term warranty or change-of-mind refund. The exact fulfilment method (new account, redemption link or existing-account upgrade) still needs owner confirmation before replacing the general delivery sentence with a specific method. "Private access" reflects the owner's stated intended offer; provider-side delivery has not been independently verified.
